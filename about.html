About us

Who We Are

MeridianCrest International is an India-based B2B exporter and sourcing partner for agricultural products, dehydrated vegetables, natural powders, spices and processed food ingredients.

What We Do

We connect international buyers with suitable Indian farmers, processors and suppliers — and coordinate the surrounding work: specification alignment, verification, documentation, buyer-specific packaging and export logistics.

How We Work

Every requirement follows the same structured path: Source → Verify → Pack → Document → Export → Deliver. Buyers always know which stage their order is at and what happens next. See the export process →

Our Mission

To make sourcing from India simpler, more transparent, and more dependable for international B2B buyers.

Our values
