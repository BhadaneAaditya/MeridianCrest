Sourcing & quality

Quality, Documentation & Compliance

A disciplined sequence — not a slogan. Each stage below applies as appropriate to your product, specification, destination and order.

1 — Supplier selection

Farmers, processors and suppliers are matched to the buyer's requirement — product, form, volume and destination — before anything is committed.

2 — Specification alignment

Form, grade, mesh/cut size, packaging, labelling and documentation scope are confirmed in writing. Unverifiable values stay Available on request.

3 — Quality verification

Checks appropriate to the product and order are coordinated; applicable documentation (such as COA, subject to order/product) is shared.

4 — Sampling

Where applicable, samples can be discussed so your QA team can evaluate before bulk commitment.

5 — Packaging checks

Bulk bags, food-grade packaging or buyer-specific packs are verified against the agreed specification, including private-label artwork where discussed.

6 — Shipment coordination

Documentation and export logistics are coordinated under the agreed shipment terms through to delivery.

Certifications & Registrations

Details available upon request.

We publish certification and registration details only for credentials the company actually holds. No certification logos are displayed until verified — this policy protects both our buyers and our credibility with customs and QA teams.

Documentation scope (per order)

Commercial invoice • Packing list • Certificate of origin • COA (subject to order/product) • Phytosanitary documentation where applicable • Other export documents where applicable. Availability depends on product, destination and order requirements.
